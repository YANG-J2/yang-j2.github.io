Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Who uses this app

Shiori is a personal app used only by its owner. It is not offered to other people.

What data the app accesses

With the owner's permission, the app accesses the owner's Google account data:

  • Gmail: reading messages and creating drafts
  • Google Calendar: reading and editing events
  • Google Tasks: reading and editing tasks
  • Google Drive: reading files

How the data is used

The data is used only to help the owner with mail, schedules and tasks. It is processed on the owner's private server. To write replies and summaries, parts of the data may be sent to the AI model provider that powers the assistant (Anthropic). The data is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with anyone else.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage and deletion

Access tokens are stored only on the owner's private server. The owner can remove the app's access at any time from the Google Account permissions page.

Contact

Questions can be sent to the user support email shown on the Google sign-in consent screen.

개인정보처리방침 (요약)

시오리는 소유자 한 명만 쓰는 앱입니다. 소유자의 허락을 받아 Gmail(읽기·초안), 캘린더, 할 일, 드라이브(읽기)에 접근하고, 소유자의 메일·일정 관리를 돕는 데만 씁니다. 데이터는 소유자의 개인 서버에서 처리되며, 답장·요약을 만들 때 일부가 AI 모델 제공자(Anthropic)로 보내질 수 있습니다. 판매·광고·제3자 공유는 하지 않습니다.
